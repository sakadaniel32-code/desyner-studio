# Desyner Studio

A free AI video editor that runs entirely in your browser. Your footage never leaves your laptop.

- Drop in a talking video and it writes out everything you say (word-level timing, done on your device).
- Click or highlight words to cut them. "Play my edit" plays the video with your cuts.
- Chat commands: "remove the ums", "make 3 shorts of 30 seconds", "make a short about pricing", "hook: 3 logo mistakes", "captions off", "make it 16:9", "undo", "export".
- Exports 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9 with word-by-word captions and a hook title. Best in Chrome or Edge on a laptop.
